\section{A joint bound across run boundaries}\label{app:joint-renewal}

The preceding pair bound can be strengthened by retaining the input pool
across a return and the next run start. For the same $B_8$ encoder at
$n=2^{21}$ and $p=2^{128}-159$, the resulting finite calculation proves
minimum distance at least $224396$ ($>10.7\%$), except with probability
less than $2^{-46.39}$ over the two independent uniform permutations.
The arithmetic remains $6.8125n-2$ field additions.

\paragraph{A common pool for neighboring cancellations.}
Fix the current quotient and a required nonzero feedback form $f$.
Let $\mu$ be the empirical distribution of the remaining active forms.
Every such form is nonzero on a surviving branch. Write
\[
 u=\mu(-f),\qquad
 v=\mu(\operatorname{span}(f)\setminus\{-f\}),\qquad w=1-u-v.
\]
Let $t=1/\tau$, where $\tau\ge1$. For three nonnegative continuation
bounds $Y,U,Z$, consider the following exposure. Reveal the next active
input. If the feedback stays nonzero, use $Y$ and stop this exposure.
If it returns to zero, reveal the next two active inputs jointly and
use $U$ or $Z$ according as their sum is nonzero or zero. The bounds are
uniform over the quotient and previous nonzero requirements.

For iid labels, the rank-weighted contribution is at most
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal H(Y,U,Z;u,v,w)
  &=(1-u)Y+wt\Psi(U,Z)+u\mathcal P(U,Z;u,v,w),\\
 \mathcal P(U,Z;u,v,w)
  &=U+t\bigl(1-(u+v)^2\bigr)Z\\
  &\quad +(Z-U)_+\max\{2uv,v^2/2\}\\
  &\quad +\bigl((Z-U)/2-tZ\bigr)_+w^2,
\end{align*}
where $a_+=\max(a,0)$ and $\Psi$ is from~\eqref{eq:fresh-pair-lemma}.
To prove this, split the first input into its mass $u$ at $-f$, its other
mass $v$ on the same line, and its independent mass $w$. A return is free
in the first case, impossible on a surviving branch in the second, and
gains one rank in the third. The independent case admits the uniform
bound $t\Psi(U,Z)$ after the quotient update. The nonzero continuation
has contribution at most $(1-u)Y$.

In the free case the quotient is unchanged. If $L,O$ are the following
pair's proportional and opposite probabilities, its contribution is at
most $U+tZ+O(Z-U)-LtZ$. On $\operatorname{span}(f)$, the proportional
mass is $(u+v)^2$ and the opposite mass is at most $\max(2uv,v^2/2)$.
Indeed, if mass $z\le v$ is at $f$, the opposite mass is at most
$2uz+(v-z)^2/2$, a convex quadratic maximized at an endpoint. Off the
line, $O_{\rm out}\le L_{\rm out}/2$ and $L_{\rm out}\le w^2$.
Dropping negative terms yields $\mathcal P$.

\paragraph{Distinct labels.}
The nonzero continuation exposes only one label, with no correction.
For a free return followed by the pair, condition the iid triple on
distinct labels, costing $m^2/((m-1)(m-2))$. For an independent return,
expose its label first; the pair on the remaining $m-1$ labels costs only
$(m-1)/(m-2)$. The original fresh pair costs $m/(m-1)$.
The budget $B(m)=\max(1,m^2)$ absorbs all three exposure lengths:
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 B(m-1)\le B(m),\qquad
 \frac m{m-1}B(m-2)\le B(m),\\
 \frac{m^2}{(m-1)(m-2)}B(m-3)\le B(m).
 \end{gathered}
\]
Use each inequality only when enough labels remain. Thus a scan with
$s$ inputs has total correction at most $s^2$, even with adaptive quotient
updates. The one-label branch does not consume the two labels used by
its alternative. Prefixes that terminate earlier are covered below.

\paragraph{Complete runs and terminal terms.}
Let $x,y>0$ mark active inputs and outputs. Choose
$\lambda>\max(1,y)$ and give each position a factor $1/\lambda$.
Summing zero-input gaps gives
\[
 A=\frac{xy}{\lambda-1},\qquad B=\frac{x}{\lambda-y}.
\]
The factor $A$ includes the nonzero output on leaving zero feedback;
$B$ reaches an active input from nonzero feedback before marking its
output. Normalize the zero-feedback potential to 1, and use $V>0$
for nonzero feedback. The homogeneous bounds are
\[
 T_0=AB\Psi(yV,1),\qquad
 T_N(u,v,w)=\mathcal H(ByV,AB^2yV,AB^2;u,v,w).
\]
For a rational $0<\epsilon<1$, require
\begin{equation}\label{eq:renewal-witness}
 T_0\le1-\epsilon,\qquad
 T_N(u,v,w)\le(1-\epsilon)V
 \quad(u,v,w\ge0,\ u+v+w=1).
\end{equation}

From zero feedback, a terminal prefix has zero or one active input before
the fresh pair completes. Its generating function is
\[
 R_0=\frac\lambda{\lambda-1}
       \left(1+\frac{xy}{\lambda-y}\right).
\]
From nonzero feedback, termination precedes the next active input or
follows a return before the next pair completes. Its contribution is at
most $R_N=\lambda/(\lambda-y)+BR_0$. Put
\[
 C=\frac{\max(R_0,R_N/V)}\epsilon.
\]
Monotonicity, positive homogeneity, and~\eqref{eq:renewal-witness} make
$C(1,V)$ a super-solution for every finite truncation of the trajectory
tree. Monotone extension of the gap sums bounds all terminal lengths.
Extracting length $n$ contributes $\lambda^n$; fixed input weight $s$
adds the correction $s^2$. Both possible final feedback states are included.

Keep the same equation span and rank potential across both scans.
For source weight $s$, intermediate weight $h$, and forbidden final
weight $e$, permutation averaging gives
\[
 \frac{F_s(\tau)}\tau
 \frac{(sh)^2 C_1\lambda_1^n C_2\lambda_2^n}
 {\binom ns\binom nh\,x_1^s(y_1x_2)^h y_2^e},
 \qquad 0<y_2\le1.
\]
The source moment $F_s$ is unchanged. The uniform continuation bounds
permit the second scan to start in the quotient left by the first.

\paragraph{Uniform rational replay.}
Numerical optimization proposes witnesses using a finite grid of pools.
This grid is not a proof of~\eqref{eq:renewal-witness}. For rational
witnesses, the verifier splits $\max(2uv,v^2/2)$ into two cubic polynomials
and homogenizes using $u+v+w=1$. On any subtriangle, the largest
degree-three Bernstein coefficient upper-bounds the polynomial. The
verifier subdivides a triangle into its four midpoint children until
both polynomials satisfy the required bound throughout every child.
It checks only rational inequalities and complete subdivisions.

At $e=224395$, the sparse logarithmic bound is $-46.396269051235$.
The full dense domain has 154 leaves: 47 use the
preceding projective-pair bound and 107 use the joint bound above.
Each leaf covers all trajectories on its weight rectangle, so selecting
either bound per leaf is valid. The same rational binomial, convex vertex,
and upward-rounded summation checks apply. The resulting total logarithmic
upper bound is $-46.396269051207$. The saved witnesses and independent
replay accompany the paper. This is a finite all-message guarantee; it
does not yet exceed the binary GV benchmark at rate $1/2$.
